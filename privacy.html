Refuge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Refuge helps you protect photos of the people you love before you share them. We built Refuge to collect as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what happens to your photos and data.

The short version

  • No accounts. We never collect your name, email, contacts, or location, we never sell your data, and your photos are never used for ads or to train anything.
  • Refuge is free and ad-supported (Google AdMob), which uses a device advertising ID. You can remove ads by subscribing or with a qualifying donation. See Advertising below.
  • The core features (removing hidden location/device data, shielding faces) run entirely on your device. Those photos never leave your phone.
  • The optional Max Protection feature uploads the single photo you choose to our processing server, applies protection, returns it to your phone, and deletes it immediately. It is never stored, logged, reviewed, or used for anything else.

What we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location. Aside from the advertising identifier used by our ads provider (see Advertising below), we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your personal data or use your photos for advertising.

On-device features (default)

When you take or choose a photo, Refuge removes hidden metadata (GPS location, timestamps, device information) and can blur detected faces. This processing happens locally on your device. These photos are not transmitted anywhere, and Refuge keeps only a temporary working copy in its private cache, which you can clear at any time.

Optional "Max Protection" (network feature)

Max Protection is off by default. If you turn it on and select a photo, that one image is uploaded over the internet to our processing server for the sole purpose of applying an AI-edit deterrent and a provenance signature. On the server, the image is:

  • processed in memory only;
  • not written to disk, not logged, and not retained after the response is returned;
  • never viewed by a person and never used to train any model or for any other purpose.

The protected image is sent back to your device and the server copy is discarded. Network requests are rate-limited and authenticated to prevent abuse.

Advertising

Refuge is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use a device advertising ID and similar identifiers, and this information is shared with Google for advertising purposes (see Google's own privacy disclosures). Where required (for example, in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first through Google's consent flow, and ads are limited to a general-audience (PG) rating. Your photos are never used for advertising, and ad providers have no access to the images you protect. You can remove ads entirely by subscribing or with a qualifying donation.

Purchases

Donations, tips, and the ad-free subscription are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo in the app.
  • Photos — only the photos you explicitly pick are accessed.
  • Notifications — to tell you when Max Protection has finished.
  • Internet — used only for the optional Max Protection feature.

Important honesty note

No tool can guarantee a photo cannot be misused once it is public. Refuge's protections are best-effort measures that make abuse harder and reduce exposure; they are not a guarantee. The strongest protection is limiting what you share publicly.

Children

Refuge is a safety tool intended for adults (such as parents and guardians) protecting photos. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Max Protection uploads are sent over an encrypted connection and processed transiently. We retain no copies. Because nothing is stored, there is nothing to breach on our side.

Your control

You can use Refuge entirely on-device by leaving Max Protection off. You can clear the app's cache or uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
