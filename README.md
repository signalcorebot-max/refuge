<p>Refuge helps you protect photos of the people you love before you share them. We built Refuge
to collect as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what happens to your photos and data.</p>

<h2>The short version</h2>
<ul>
  <li>No accounts. No ads. No analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share your data.</li>
  <li>The core features (removing hidden location/device data, shielding faces) run <strong>entirely on your device</strong>. Those photos never leave your phone.</li>
  <li>The optional <strong>Max Protection</strong> feature uploads the single photo you choose to our processing server, applies protection, returns it to your phone, and <strong>deletes it immediately</strong>. It is never stored, logged, reviewed, or used for anything else.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What we do <em>not</em> collect</h2>
<p>We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, device
identifiers, or usage analytics. We do not use advertising or third-party trackers.</p>

<h2>On-device features (default)</h2>
<p>When you take or choose a photo, Refuge removes hidden metadata (GPS location, timestamps, device
information) and can blur detected faces. This processing happens locally on your device. These photos
are not transmitted anywhere, and Refuge keeps only a temporary working copy in its private cache,
which you can clear at any time.</p>

<h2>Optional "Max Protection" (network feature)</h2>
<p>Max Protection is off by default. If you turn it on and select a photo, that one image is uploaded
over the internet to our processing server for the sole purpose of applying an AI-edit deterrent and a
provenance signature. On the server, the image is:</p>
<ul>
  <li>processed <strong>in memory only</strong>;</li>
  <li><strong>not written to disk, not logged, and not retained</strong> after the response is returned;</li>
  <li><strong>never viewed by a person</strong> and never used to train any model or for any other purpose.</li>
</ul>
<p>The protected image is sent back to your device and the server copy is discarded. Network requests
are rate-limited and authenticated to prevent abuse.</p>

<h2>Permissions</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Camera</strong> — only when you choose to take a photo in the app.</li>
  <li><strong>Photos</strong> — only the photos you explicitly pick are accessed.</li>
  <li><strong>Notifications</strong> — to tell you when Max Protection has finished.</li>
  <li><strong>Internet</strong> — used only for the optional Max Protection feature.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Important honesty note</h2>
<p>No tool can guarantee a photo cannot be misused once it is public. Refuge's protections are
<strong>best-effort</strong> measures that make abuse harder and reduce exposure; they are not a
guarantee. The strongest protection is limiting what you share publicly.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>Refuge is a safety tool intended for adults (such as parents and guardians) protecting photos. It is
not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>

<h2>Data security</h2>
<p>Max Protection uploads are sent over an encrypted connection and processed transiently. We retain
no copies. Because nothing is stored, there is nothing to breach on our side.</p>

<h2>Your control</h2>
<p>You can use Refuge entirely on-device by leaving Max Protection off. You can clear the app's cache
or uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data.</p>

<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.</p>
