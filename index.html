What Refuge does

Strips hidden data

Removes GPS location, timestamps, and device info — right on your phone, before you share.

Shields faces

Detects faces and can blur them so they can't be easily lifted and reused.

AI-Edit Shield

An optional, best-effort layer that disrupts AI “nudify” and deepfake editors.

Real guidance

Honest, practical steps to protect your family — plus one-tap links to Take It Down & StopNCII.

We keep nothing

The basic protections run entirely on your phone — those photos never leave your device. The optional Max Protection sends one photo over an encrypted connection, applies protection, sends it right back, and deletes it immediately. Never stored, never logged, never used to train anything.

The only one of its kind

The research behind this kind of protection has mostly lived in universities and labs, on desktop computers. As far as we know, Refuge is the only app that puts it in everyday hands — something you can use in seconds, right from your phone.

Honest by design

No tool can guarantee a photo is safe once it's public. Refuge's protections are best-effort — they make abuse harder and reduce exposure. The strongest protection is limiting what you share publicly. We tell you the truth so you can make good choices.
