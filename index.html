What Refuge does

We keep nothing

The basic protections run entirely on your phone — those photos never leave your device. The optional Max Protection sends one photo over an encrypted connection, applies protection, sends it right back, and deletes it immediately. Never stored, never logged, never used to train anything.

Honest by design

No tool can guarantee a photo is safe once it's public. Refuge's protections are best-effort — they make abuse harder and reduce exposure. The strongest protection is limiting what you share publicly. We tell you the truth so you can make good choices.
